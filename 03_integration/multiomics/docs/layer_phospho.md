## What to keep in mind

- Three cohorts, each in two normalisations. The two arms are reported separately and cohorts are counted rather than arms, so running both never double-counts a cohort.
- The protein-adjusted arm concentrates on pathways but never agrees on a direction across all three cohorts; the raw arm does, and that directional signal matches the proteome's own.
