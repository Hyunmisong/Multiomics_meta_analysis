## What to keep in mind

- Eight cohorts, the deepest layer. Call rates range from 14% (PG1) to 88% (PE5) of the genes each one measures, which is why the null gives every cohort its own flagging rate rather than assuming a shared one.
- PE3 ships direction calls with no effect size, so the fold-change schemes collapse to that call for it.
